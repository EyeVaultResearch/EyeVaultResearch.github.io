Data visualizations

Three views of the same survey

These charts explore the 109-dataset index from a different angle: how completely participant attributes get reported as the field has grown, where individual datasets sit on year versus completeness, and which named tracker models show up when.

← Back to dataset index

Reporting over time

Percentage of that year's datasets reporting a chosen participant attribute. Small yearly totals (shown in the tooltip) can make early or sparse years noisy — a single dataset either reports something or it doesn't.

Reporting by category

The same 21 participant attributes, sliced by a study characteristic instead of by year. Darker cells mean a larger share of that group reports the attribute; hover a cell for exact counts.

Dataset landscape

Each dataset placed by publication year and how completely it reports participant attributes. Bubble size is the number of participants; color is dataset availability. Select a bubble to open that dataset in the index.

Explore the data

Pick any two measures for the axes and a study characteristic to color by. Select a point to open that dataset in the index.

Eye-tracker timeline

When the most-used, explicitly named tracker models appear in the collection. Dot size reflects how many datasets used that model in a given year.

Built from the EyeVault survey of 109 eye tracking datasets published or utilised in ETRA proceedings, 2000–2023.
